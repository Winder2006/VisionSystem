How it works

  1. Show the camera a thumbs up and hold the first button for a few seconds.
  2. Show a thumbs down and hold the second button.
  3. Let go and watch it guess. Add more classes or examples to teach it more.
Everything runs in your browser. Your camera feed never leaves your device.
